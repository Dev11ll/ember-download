Questions

Is Ember really free?

Yes. No price, no account, no watermark and no ads.

Can I get banned for using it?

Ember doesn't inject anything into games or read their memory. It captures your screen and audio the same way OBS and Discord screen sharing do.

Will it slow down my games?

Ember records with the video encoder built into your graphics card, which is separate from the part that runs your game. Most players notice no difference. On an older PC, pick the Standard or Low quality preset.

Where do my clips go?

To a folder on your PC (Videos\Ember by default, or any folder you choose). Nothing is uploaded unless you press Share and create a link.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is that normal?

Yes, Windows shows this for new apps that haven't built up a reputation yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. Always download Ember from this page so you know it's the real installer.

Do I need to install anything else?

No. The installer has everything. The first time it starts, Ember downloads its recorder (FFmpeg) automatically.

Who can see my share links?

Anyone who has the link can watch that clip. Links aren't listed or searchable anywhere, and nothing is shared unless you choose to.

What do I need?

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). A graphics card from the last few years is recommended for recording without slowing your game.
